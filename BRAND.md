# LITSA identity: terms of use

The code in this repository is MIT licensed. The following are not:

- the LITSA torch mark, in every variant
- the LITSA wordmark
- the LITSA Display typeface

These identify the Legal Innovation and Technology Student Association at Suffolk University Law School. Use them only for LITSA's own projects, events and materials, or with written permission from LITSA's president.

You may show them unchanged when referring to LITSA, for example in a news article or a partner's event listing.
